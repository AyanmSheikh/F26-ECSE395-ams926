## 2. Prototype responsibilities and sequence

Ayan and Joe will continue Prototype 1, the placement sketches and layout. Daniel will develop Prototype 2, the cardboard mockup. Emmett will work on the electronics integration for Prototype 3. The layout and physical build will need to agree with the electronics arrangement.

The drawings need to establish where the parts go and how the equipment sits relative to the feeder, window, and bushes. The cardboard stage will let us check that arrangement at full size. As it develops, Prototype 2 will include motion indication, a camera view, and a manually operated light stand-in. Prototype 3 will bring the detection, control, and output hardware together inside the mockup. The more finished enclosure comes after those placement and operating questions have been worked through.

We have not reached that integrated stage yet. Camera setup is still pending, but we can continue the placement drawings and mockup work while the electronics are being prepared. The physical layout should also leave room to connect, remove, and service the boards instead of assuming that every component can be permanently fixed in place.

## 3. What the drawings and mockup need to show

We discussed the feeder's position outside Mr. Fong's window and the space he uses to remove and rehang it. The layout needs to show the enclosure footprint, component positions, the intended camera view, and access for refilling and servicing. The drawings therefore need to include the feeder and the window-side access, not only the box.

The camera has to see the feeding region and an approaching target without the feeder or its own enclosure blocking the useful view. We also need to keep the equipment out of the way of Mr. Fong's arm and preserve his view of the feeder. The drawings will give Daniel a layout to carry into the cardboard mockup and give Emmett a physical arrangement to work toward when connecting the hardware.

Mounting position and clearance remained questions to work through in the afternoon lab. We did not select a final mounting arrangement in this morning meeting. The immediate task was to compare placement against the feeder, window-side access, and the space needed by the camera and other components before committing to the next physical version.
