We discussed the feeder's position outside Mr. Fong's window and the space he uses to remove and rehang it. The layout needs to show the enclosure footprint, component positions, the intended camera view, and access for refilling and servicing. The drawings therefore need to include the feeder and the window-side access, not only the box.

The camera has to see the feeding region and an approaching target without the feeder or its own enclosure blocking the useful view. We also need to keep the equipment out of the way of Mr. Fong's arm and preserve his view of the feeder. The drawings will give Daniel a layout to carry into the cardboard mockup and give Emmett a physical arrangement to work toward when connecting the hardware.

Mounting position and clearance remained questions to work through in the afternoon lab. We did not select a final mounting arrangement in this morning meeting.
